# Demographic Aging and Economic Growth

This project studies how population aging relates to economic growth in Europe and Africa. Using country-level data, we plan to compare the relationship between changes in the age structure and growth in GDP per capita across the two regions. We also aim to examine the roles of employment and labor productivity, and to explore whether the relationship varies with automation where data are available.

The analysis draws on two papers:

- Maestas, Mullen, and Powell (2023), *The Effect of Population Aging on Economic Growth, the Labor Force, and Productivity*.
- Acemoglu and Restrepo (2017), *Secular Stagnation? The Effect of Aging on Economic Growth in the Age of Automation*.

The project is in progress.
